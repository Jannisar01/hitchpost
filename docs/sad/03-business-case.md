**What changed:** earlier versions said platforms "could ship" agent access. They already have. Risk R-02 has materialized (section 4). Every rival's agent access ends in its own silo, and every rival either bundles bookings into a flat subscription or charges only for new clients. Returning clients are free everywhere ([30](30-competitive-heuristic-evaluation.md) section 5).

### What's left open

Nobody I've found offers these together: a neutral network of agent lines that any vendor's agent can reach, for people as well as businesses; verified identity for agents and their owners; a relationship graph with sharing rules per relationship; and an approval record both sides can check. That's my position. The booking slice is checked against verified rivals above; the social-network slice stays a hypothesis until the scan is extended (A-07).

## 2. Business model

### Options

| Option                                      | Who pays                    | For what                                                       | Pros                                                             | Cons                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A. Business agent line subscription         | Business                    | A verified business agent line: listing, hosting, booking sync | Predictable; matches how businesses already pay; nothing to game | Hard sell before there's demand                                     |
| B. Per-booking fee                          | Business                    | Each honored booking                                           | Pays only for results                                            | Rewards under-marking outcomes; charges for the business's regulars |
| C. Consumer subscription                    | Person                      | Personal agent and app                                         | Clear value if the app is great                                  | Consumer subscriptions are hard; competes with general assistants   |
| D. Open core plus hosted platform services  | Platforms and developers    | Paid verification and attestation API                          | Fits MIT and the open protocol                                   | Needs scale to matter                                               |
| E. Platform fee on paid agent subscriptions | Subscriber, via the creator | A share of paid subscriptions to consult a person's agent      | Grows with the network; aligned with creators                    | Needs payments, KYC and the social layers first (stage L4)          |

**My choice for v1: A**, with people always free. Five expert panels independently found that B (the earlier hypothesis) makes a business pay for its own regulars and rewards it for marking bookings as no-shows, since the business marks the outcome it would be billed on. A flat fee removes both problems. D and E are later revenue lines. C stays off the table: the built-in personal agent is how I solve cold start, not a product to charge for.

### Price hypotheses (to test, not to announce)

One set of hypotheses, used everywhere in this package, including the revenue model in [02 Feasibility study](02-feasibility-study.md):

- **People: free**, for their own agent line and contacts.
- **Business agent line: $15 per verified business agent line per month** (P-26), free for the whole pilot. Outcome marking is never billed.
- **Anchor:** published rival subscriptions run $19.95 to $30 per month (section 1). Hitchpost sits in front of the business's existing tool rather than replacing it, so it has to cost less than that tool.
- **Platforms and developers:** the hitchpost/1 extension, its JSON Schemas and the conformance kit are open; listing their A2A endpoints in the directory is free. A paid verification and attestation API is a later line (option D).
- **Paid agent subscriptions (stage L4): a 10% platform fee** on subscriptions people buy to consult another person's or business's agent (P-30). This is a hypothesis for a stage that isn't designed yet. It needs a payments provider, payee KYC, 1099-K reporting, the FTC negative-option ("click to cancel") rule and a refund policy before it can exist.
- **Tested by money, not opinion:** at pilot stage S1 each business signs a letter of intent or pays a refundable $10 deposit toward the first month after the pilot (A-05).

## 3. Assumptions and how I'll test them

Each assumption gets the cheapest test that could prove it wrong, and a decision rule set **before** the test runs. Small samples get confidence intervals and a three-way band (continue, iterate, kill), not a single line.

| ID       | Assumption                                                                                                          | Cheapest test                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Decision rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Before                                               |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **A-01** | People will book through an agent and approve offers, instead of tapping the app they already use or calling        | Disclosed Wizard-of-Oz for **10 weeks** with **30 people**: participants are told "a person operates the agent in this study". A scripted agent persona with fixed response latency. Strangers recruited through a screener, no friends or family. A neutral facilitator where possible. Signed consent; research data kept 90 days after the study (P-27). Ten weeks covers at least two booking cycles for someone who books every 3 to 4 weeks ([04](04-stakeholders-and-personas.md)) | Pre-registered. **Continue** if the lower bound of the 95% confidence interval is at least 50% for one completed booking and at least 20% for two. **Kill** if the upper bound is under 40% for one booking. **Iterate** in between. First-offer approval and time-to-approve are reported with intervals, separately from agent response time | M-01, before Phase 2 build beyond protocol-safe work |
| **A-02** | Businesses will list and act on bookings that arrive this way                                                       | Interview 10 local barbers about past behaviour (which tool, last missed booking, last tool they paid for), not hypothetical "would you" questions                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Fewer than 3 of 10 show a recent, costly missed-booking problem: pivot the beachhead                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Phase 3 starts                                       |
| **A-03** | Businesses' booking tools allow programmatic reads and writes                                                       | Desk audit of public APIs and terms for the tools named in A-02. Square's Bookings API is already confirmed public ([30](30-competitive-heuristic-evaluation.md))                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | None of the top 3 tools allows writes. Pivot: Hitchpost hosts the book, or calendar sync                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Phase 3 starts                                       |
| **A-04** | Agent booking is faster for the person than the app they already use                                                | Time-to-book baseline at A-01 enrolment, against the person's current app or phone, owner excluded; then the same with the Wizard-of-Oz flow                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Not at least 50% faster end to end, measured as approval time plus agent latency reported separately                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | M-01                                                 |
| **A-05** | A neutral network is defensible and businesses will pay for an agent line                                           | Verified competitor scan (done for booking in [30](30-competitive-heuristic-evaluation.md)), plus a letter of intent or a refundable $10 deposit at pilot stage S1                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Fewer than 3 pilot businesses sign or pay: reprice or rethink option A                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | M-02, and again at S1                                |
| **A-06** | People trust reviews from verified interactions more than ordinary reviews                                          | 5 short interviews showing two listing mockups                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | No preference                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Phase 5 starts                                       |
| **A-07** | Nobody offers a neutral, cross-vendor network of agent lines with verified identity and a two-sided approval record | Extend [30](30-competitive-heuristic-evaluation.md) beyond booking platforms to agent directories and assistant vendors, with primary sources and dates                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | A live product covers all of it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Phase 3 starts                                       |
| **A-08** | The built-in personal agent is enough on the consumer side at launch, without third-party assistants                | Measure in the A-01 test whether people care which assistant they use                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Most people refuse a new app: move the MCP gateway (stage L3) earlier                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Phase 4 starts                                       |

The three riskiest are **A-01**, **A-02 with A-03**, and **A-05**. None of them needs code to test. Assumptions for the social stages (will people let other agents consult theirs, will anyone pay to consult an agent) are written when those stages are designed.

## 5. Go-to-market: one Texas neighbourhood

The full plan is [29 Go-to-market plan](29-go-to-market-plan.md). The business-case view:

1. **Pick one area** in Texas with at least 15 barbershops within walking distance, where I can visit in person.
2. **Recruit from the A-02 interviews.** Free agent line and setup for the first 10 businesses for the whole pilot, done by me in person. Keep interviewed businesses warm monthly until the pilot starts.
3. **Seed demand** from the A-01 participants, then their friends, with the app's person-to-person contacts (UC-11) as a reason to install it beyond one barber. Target 50 people who've booked at least once.
4. **Measure weekly:** north star, counter-metrics, listing time, reports.
5. **Ask for money at S1:** letters of intent or refundable deposits (A-05).
6. **Decide at 12 weeks:** expand to the next neighbourhood, change the beachhead, or stop. The criteria are the decision rules in section 3 and the v1 release criteria in [01](01-vision-and-scope.md). Each further neighbourhood gets the same 12-week rule; the counts in [02](02-feasibility-study.md) section 4.2 assume two such expansions and drop out if a step fails.
