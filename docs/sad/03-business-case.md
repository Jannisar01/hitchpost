This was the business case. Since round 5 Hitchpost is **protocol first: nobody pays**, so this document is the **adoption case**: why people, developers and businesses would take up a free, open protocol that lets their agent talk to other agents directly, what has to be true for that to happen, and how I'll find out cheaply before building too far. It measures adoption, not revenue. Booking is the first task type the protocol has to prove ([01 Vision and scope](01-vision-and-scope.md)). The file keeps its name so links don't break.

**What changed:** earlier versions said platforms "could ship" agent access. They already have. Risk R-02 has materialized (section 4). Every rival's agent access ends in its own silo. That is the opening for a free, open protocol: a business with a Fresha or Square agent should be reachable by anyone's assistant, not only by the platform's chosen partners ([30](30-competitive-heuristic-evaluation.md) section 5).

### What's left open

Nobody I've found offers these together: an open protocol and a neutral network of agent lines that any vendor's agent can reach, for people as well as businesses; verified identity for agents and their owners; a relationship graph with sharing rules per relationship; and an approval record both sides can check. That's my position. The booking slice is checked against verified rivals above; the network slice stays a hypothesis until the scan is extended (A-07).

## 2. Adoption model

### Who adopts, and why

| Adopter                                                               | What they get                                                                                                          | What it costs them                         | Phase      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| **AI power users** (people who already run Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) | Their own assistant can find an agent line and book through it, via the minimal MCP gateway, without a new app         | Nothing; an MCP server they add            | 3          |
| **Agent developers** (Priya)                                          | An open extension, JSON Schemas and a conformance kit; their agent becomes reachable by every assistant on the network | Nothing; MIT code                          | 2 (kit), 3 |
| **Businesses** (Marcus)                                               | A free agent line in front of the booking tool they already use; bookings arrive with an approval record               | Nothing; setup done with them in the pilot | 2 to 6     |
| **People without an assistant** (Dana, sometimes)                     | The built-in personal agent in the app; contacts are agent lines, people or businesses                                 | Nothing                                    | 4          |

**First users are AI power users and agent developers.** They self-serve, already know MCP, and are the ones who will build agents for the network. Businesses join because it's free. Barbers are the first business demonstration, reached where possible through agents they already have (Square, Fresha and the like). The built-in app serves people who don't run an assistant; it is no longer the only demand channel.

### Monetization: Later and undecided

**Nobody pays, by design.** There is no price, fee, subscription price or platform fee anywhere in the current plan, and no willingness-to-pay test. The earlier business-line price (P-26) and platform fee (P-30) are retired ([00 §10.1](00-document-control.md#101-parameter-register)). If adoption ever warrants it, possible future options include hosted agent lines for people and businesses who don't run their own, and verification services for platforms. They carry no numbers and no dates. Subscriptions to consult an agent (stage L4) stay Later and unpriced.

## 3. Assumptions and how I'll test them

Each assumption gets the cheapest test that could prove it wrong, and a decision rule set **before** the test runs. Small samples get confidence intervals and a three-way band (continue, iterate, kill), not a single line.

| ID       | Assumption                                                                                                          | Cheapest test                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Decision rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Before                                               |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **A-01** | People will book through an agent and approve offers, instead of tapping the app they already use or calling        | Disclosed Wizard-of-Oz for **10 weeks** with **30 people**: participants are told "a person operates the agent in this study". A scripted agent persona with fixed response latency. Strangers recruited through a screener, no friends or family. A neutral facilitator where possible. Signed consent; research data kept 90 days after the study (P-27). Ten weeks covers at least two booking cycles for someone who books every 3 to 4 weeks ([04](04-stakeholders-and-personas.md)) | Pre-registered. **Continue** if the lower bound of the 95% confidence interval is at least 50% for one completed booking and at least 20% for two. **Kill** if the upper bound is under 40% for one booking. **Iterate** in between. First-offer approval and time-to-approve are reported with intervals, separately from agent response time. The results re-size release criterion 2 in [01](01-vision-and-scope.md) | M-01, before Phase 2 build beyond protocol-safe work |
| **A-02** | Businesses will list and act on bookings that arrive this way                                                       | Interview 10 local barbers about past behaviour (which tool, last missed booking, whether their tool already exposes an agent), not hypothetical "would you" questions                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Fewer than 3 of 10 show a recent, costly missed-booking problem: change the first business demonstration                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Phase 3 starts                                       |
| **A-03** | Businesses' booking tools allow programmatic reads and writes                                                       | Desk audit of public APIs and terms for the tools named in A-02. Square's Bookings API is already confirmed public ([30](30-competitive-heuristic-evaluation.md))                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | None of the top 3 tools allows writes. Pivot: Hitchpost hosts the book, or calendar sync                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Phase 3 starts                                       |
| **A-04** | Agent booking is faster for the person than the app they already use                                                | Time-to-book baseline at A-01 enrolment, against the person's current app or phone, owner excluded; then the same with the Wizard-of-Oz flow                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Not at least 50% faster end to end, measured as approval time plus agent latency reported separately                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | M-01                                                 |
| **A-05** | A free, neutral protocol gets adopted: businesses keep a live agent line and developers build on it                 | Verified competitor scan (done for booking in [30](30-competitive-heuristic-evaluation.md)); count pilot businesses whose listing stays live through S1; count independent implementations that pass the conformance kit                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Fewer than 3 pilot businesses keep a live listing through S1, or no independent implementation passes the kit by Phase 6: rethink the protocol's value before building the social stages                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | M-02, again at S1 and at Phase 6                     |
| **A-06** | People trust reviews from verified interactions more than ordinary reviews                                          | 5 short interviews showing two listing mockups                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | No preference                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Phase 5 starts                                       |
| **A-07** | Nobody offers a neutral, cross-vendor network of agent lines with verified identity and a two-sided approval record | Extend [30](30-competitive-heuristic-evaluation.md) beyond booking platforms to agent directories and assistant vendors, with primary sources and dates                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | A live product covers all of it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Phase 3 starts                                       |
| **A-08** | People who run their own assistant will book through the minimal MCP gateway instead of a new app                   | In A-01, record which assistant each participant already uses and whether they'd add an MCP server; in the pilot, count bookings that arrive through the gateway                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Fewer than 10 bookings through the gateway by the end of S2 (release criterion 2): revisit the gateway's tools and onboarding before L1                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Phase 3 starts, again at S1                          |

The three riskiest are **A-01**, **A-02 with A-03**, and **A-05**. None of them needs code to test. Assumptions for the social stages (will people let other agents consult theirs, will anyone want to subscribe to an agent) are written when those stages are designed.

## 5. Go-to-market: developers and power users first, one Texas neighbourhood for businesses

The full plan is [29 Go-to-market plan](29-go-to-market-plan.md). The adoption view:

1. **Open the protocol to developers first.** Publish the `hitchpost/1` extension, JSON Schemas and conformance kit (Phase 2) in A2A and MCP developer channels; invite agent developers to make their agents reachable.
2. **Reach AI power users through their own assistants.** The minimal MCP gateway (Phase 3) lets anyone's Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini find an agent line and book through it, with an explicit confirmation step.
3. **Pick one area** in Texas with at least 15 barbershops within walking distance, where I can visit in person. Recruit from the A-02 interviews; agent lines are free, and setup is done with them in person. Keep interviewed businesses warm monthly until the pilot starts.
4. **Seed demand** from the A-01 participants and power users in the area, with the app's person-to-person contacts (UC-11) for people without an assistant.
5. **Measure weekly:** north star, counter-metrics, listing time, bookings through the gateway, reports.
6. **Kill check at S1:** if release criterion 2 in [01](01-vision-and-scope.md) is out of reach, the decision rules in section 3 apply before more is built.
7. **Decide at 12 weeks:** expand to the next neighbourhood, change the first business demonstration, or stop. Each further neighbourhood gets the same 12-week rule.
